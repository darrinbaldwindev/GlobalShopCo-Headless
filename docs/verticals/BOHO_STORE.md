# GlobalShopCo Boho Store

## Status

NON-PRODUCTION / RESEARCH AND HEADLESS PREPARATION ONLY

## Purpose

Define the Boho fashion vertical for GlobalShopCo as a specialist headless storefront backed by the canonical Shopify catalogue.

## Architecture

- Shopify remains the sole product, variant, pricing, inventory, cart and checkout source of truth.
- The Boho storefront is a WordPress/headless merchandising and presentation layer only.
- The Boho storefront must not create or maintain a duplicate product catalogue or independent commerce authority.
- Customer purchase initiation must hand off to the approved Shopify commerce/checkout flow.

## Initial Merchandising Scope

Prioritise:
- flowing maxi dresses;
- wide-leg / palazzo pants;
- kaftans;
- kimonos;
- loose cotton/rayon tops;
- wrap/tiered skirts;
- boho accessories where commercially suitable.

Initially avoid high sizing/return-risk categories such as fitted jeans, bodycon dresses, bras, swimwear and heavily tailored garments unless later evidence supports them.

## Product Qualification Gate

No product is launch-ready merely because it looks suitable for the Boho store. For each candidate, preserve evidence for:
1. supplier and exact product/SKU;
2. GTIN/EAN where available;
3. AU stock or realistic AU delivery SLA;
4. Shopify compatibility or a clear supplier/import path;
5. wholesale/dropship cost and GST basis where available;
6. delivery/freight and free-delivery economics;
7. competitor retail pricing;
8. expected contribution after payment fees and a returns reserve;
9. sizing complexity and return risk;
10. returns/warranty terms;
11. product/content/image reuse rights where relevant;
12. supplier reliability and fulfilment identity;
13. explicit UNKNOWNs and confidence.

Products whose commercial viability depends on unknown freight or supplier terms remain HOLD / UNKNOWN.

## First Research Mission

The Boho Overseer must identify and screen 50 exact candidate products for the Australian market before recommending a first launch cohort.

The 50-product set should favour:
- Australian-stock or fast-AU-delivery candidates;
- distinctive boho styling rather than generic mass-catalogue duplication;
- forgiving sizing and lower return risk;
- compact/light products where possible;
- sufficient retail spread to support GlobalShopCo free delivery;
- reliable suppliers with clear Shopify or operational integration paths.

## First Launch Cohort Gate

After 50-product research, shortlist the strongest 5–10 evidence-complete products. No production publication or paid promotion occurs until the normal GlobalShopCo site-wide launch gate is satisfied.

## Governance

- Research and documentation may proceed autonomously.
- No supplier contact, purchase, app installation, credential change, production Shopify mutation, publication, deployment, merge, financial commitment or marketplace listing without explicit authority.
- No overall GREEN may be claimed by the Boho Overseer.
- Evidence outranks claims. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.
- Significant work must be logged durably through the existing GlobalShopCo / headless GitHub workflow.
